# Native AVX-512 model timing prototype

A private AVX-512 prototype showed median paired-ratio estimates that imply 2.5% to 5.0% time reductions in four native CLI inference cases. The result comes from one busy Linux host. It does not establish browser or GPU latency, broad workload gains, or production readiness.

## Results

For each quartet, the ratio is the baseline arm median divided by the candidate arm median. The table reports the median of those eight paired ratios. A ratio above 1 favors the candidate. The implied paired time reduction is `1 - 1 / ratio`. Each range is the minimum to maximum of eight paired ratios, not a confidence interval.

| Model and request | Median of paired ratios | Implied paired time reduction | Quartets favoring candidate | Paired ratio range |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Laya Q8, short | 1.02596 | 2.53% | 7 of 8 | 0.93046 to 1.04912 |
| Laya Q8, long | 1.03519 | 3.40% | 7 of 8 | 0.99343 to 1.09106 |
| Kev-0.8B Q8, short | 1.02605 | 2.54% | 6 of 8 | 0.97974 to 1.12623 |
| Kev-0.8B Q8, long | 1.05282 | 5.02% | 8 of 8 | 1.01169 to 1.16510 |

Three ranges include 1. Separate A/A paired-ratio medians were 0.99853, 1.01073, 0.99644, and 1.00731. The audit applied no timing correction or outlier exclusion. The small sample does not establish a reliable gain across similar requests.

## Method and limits

The four fixed requests used Laya Q8 and Kev-0.8B Q8 with 35-character and 629-character inputs. The run recorded 304 fresh exact responses and audited 1,520 numeric values. It measured `Model::decide` after model load and input parsing, before response serialization. Printed timing resolution was 0.1 ms. Model load and total request time were separate observations.

The run used logical CPU 7 on a busy host. The actual default remained tile 2. These are native CLI CPU timings. They are not browser latency, GPU latency, or end-to-end loading measurements. The results cover four requests and do not support a model ranking or a general speed claim.

The baseline source was commit `8e4ae3a3ff6592e9581696057f07fa90e036e10a`. Its `crates/kevala/src/kernels.rs` SHA-256 was `a1d101ed08726cafb2d7a301a39edf789adfc203044852fd8c7b4cf7cf12b5da`. The private candidate overlay used kernel SHA-256 `d43c91d9bc1b90f05d6ec870f90c8b11c5ca5adc8d00bf598eb4145fc2958d0e` and `crates/kevala/build.rs` SHA-256 `443856de805d5d967c2f730cd4f5149b84523adff4181e8868efef999f1f9734`.

A separate nine-frame prototype gate passed its recorded checks. Its code generation summary counted a 20-instruction ZMM inner loop. That count excludes helper setup, panic handling, and `vzeroupper`. The gate does not validate the production dispatch source or the current release build.

Production dispatch has not been built, benchmarked, or release-validated. The experiment did not change the default. No production promotion follows from these measurements.
