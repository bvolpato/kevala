Gemma RMS storage-barrier guard

This model-free DEV guard runs at D=1536 and compares each registered RMS residual/norm and scale/next shader against a candidate with only its storageBarrier() line removed. The update loop, second-moment Y reload, reductions, and H output remain byte-identical. It checks complete Y and H buffers bit-for-bit, including signed zero, finiteness, and tail sentinels, before and after timestamped comparisons.

It covers T=2, 12, and 97; modes 0, 1, and 2; normal, signed-zero, cancellation, and large-finite inputs. The default uses workgroup reductions. Add ?subgroups=1 to request the optional exact-32-lane subgroup path; an unsupported feature is reported as skipped with success:false.
