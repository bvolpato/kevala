Suffix paired F16 BM112 tile

Dev-only Q8/F16 comparison of the current paired R4/BM64/BN64 kernel against a 112-row paired tile with 14 independent accumulators per thread. The candidate retains the original K32 dot order, F16 operand conversions, virtual BM64/BN64 split policy, and gate/up partial-plane layout. Its 15 KiB workgroup tile loads each weight tile once.

Checks every activation and both complete partial planes by bits, including signed zeros, padding, and tails before and after alternating paired timestamps. Controlled temporary host/GPU allocations are capped at 64 MiB with 1 MiB staged reads; immutable weights are reported separately. Requires shader-f16 and requests F16 and timestamps only.

Use ?f16=1&shape=gate6144&T=97 for the primary timing, or omit the shape filter for both actual gate widths and ragged/zero/cancellation/saturation guards. Add ?boundaries=1 for row-tile boundary cases.
