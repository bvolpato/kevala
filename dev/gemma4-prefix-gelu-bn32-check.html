Fast prefix BN32 gate/up/GELU fusion

Dev-only F32 Q8 R2 probe against two registered BN32 projections, their reducers, and registered GELU. Checks full activation and both partial-plane bits, signed zeros, padding, and tails before and after paired timings. All controlled host/GPU allocations fit 64 MiB. Use ?bounds=all for every row count from 1 through 32 or ?wide=1 for the optional 12288-wide MLP.
