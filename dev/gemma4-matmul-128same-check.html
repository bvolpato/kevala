WG128 BM56 matmul guard

Dev-only F32 Q8 probe. It compares the registered 256-thread BM56/BN64 kernel with a 128-thread BM56/BN64 candidate. Both retain the virtual BM64/BN64 split policy and the same eight ordered K/4 dot steps; the candidate assigns fourteen vec2 accumulators (28 scalar outputs) to each thread.

At T=97 it checks full output and split-partial raw bits, all three output modes, bias on/off, and sentinel tails before and after paired GPU timestamp measurements. It covers the Gemma 4 gate, down, Q, and O projections plus a ragged N/K shape. Temporary allocations are capped at 64 MiB, excluding immutable weights.
