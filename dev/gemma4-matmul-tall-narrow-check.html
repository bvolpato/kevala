F32 Q8 tall, narrow matmul

Dev-only comparison of the current BM56/BN64 suffix matmul against BM112/BN32. The candidate uses fourteen scalar accumulators per thread, the original ascending K32 dot order, split partitions, epilogue and reducer. Shared storage is 18,432 bytes, checked against the device limit.

The primary case is T97, N6144, K1536. Use ?all=1 for gate, down and Q; ?guards=1 adds ragged, signed-zero, cancellation and unsplit fixtures. Full output and partial-buffer bits, finite values, padding and tails are checked for all six mode/bias combinations before and after three alternating timestamp pairs with four repetitions. Controlled host/GPU temporary memory stays below 64 MiB; immutable weights are reported separately.
