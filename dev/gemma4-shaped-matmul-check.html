Gemma F32 shape-constant matmul guard

Dev-only Q8/F32 comparison. Each candidate is the registered Fast-prefix or suffix matmul shader with only N and K compiled as constants. The reducer stays identical. Checks full output and split partial bits for all three modes, bias off/on, and tails, then reports three alternating timestamp pairs with four repetitions per path. Temporary host and GPU buffers stay under 64 MiB, excluding immutable weight and scale buffers.

Opening this page starts the benchmark, but it loads no model or published weight pack. Optional filters: ?scope=fast or ?scope=suffix, and &shape=gate, down, q, o, or ragged.
