F32 paired K16 staging

Dev-only Q8/F32 BM56 comparison: current separate gate/up projections, registered paired K32, and paired K16 staging. The candidate retains ordered vec4 dots, K32 quantization scales, split partitions and the existing GELU reducer, while using 12 KiB of shared memory.

Defaults to T97, N6144, K1536, S3. Use ?guards=1 for ragged, signed-zero, cancellation and saturation cases; ?shape=gate12288 checks the unsplit wide shape. Full raw-bit outputs, partials, inactive padding and tails are checked before and after timing. Controlled temporary allocations stay within 64 MiB; resident immutable weights are reported separately.
